How the model works

Target pot: the amount needed at retirement to cover the desired income after subtracting State Pension income, discounted using your assumed post-retirement growth rate. State Pension is modelled as £12,548 a year in today's money, available from age 67, and increasing by 2.5% each year.

Contribution estimate: solves for a level starting monthly contribution that grows with your selected assumption and builds the target pot by retirement. If inflation-linked contributions are enabled, contributions increase once per year by the inflation assumption.

Important: this is an illustrative projection, not financial advice. It excludes tax, fees, annuity pricing, contribution limits, and changes to State Pension policy. Returns are smooth assumptions; real investment returns vary. State Pension is shown gross and the income goal is treated as a gross amount.
